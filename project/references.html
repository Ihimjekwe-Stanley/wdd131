Project Information

Stanway Poultry Feed Guide is a WDD131 Web Development project created to provide simple and practical information about poultry feed, feeding practices, common ingredients, and feed cost management.

The information on this website is intended for educational purposes. Farmers should consider their local conditions, available ingredients, bird health, and professional recommendations when making feeding decisions.
