How to Choose the Right Feed

Poultry feed should match the age, purpose, and production stage of the birds. Young birds generally require feed that supports development, while mature birds have different nutritional needs.

Farmers should also consider feed quality, ingredient availability, storage conditions, bird health, and the cost of feeding when choosing a feed program.
