Practical Poultry Feeding Tips

Good feeding is more than simply giving birds feed. Proper management can help reduce waste, support healthy birds, and improve farm performance.

1. Use the Right Feed

Choose feed according to the bird's age, type, and production stage. Broilers, layers, and cockerels have different nutritional needs.

2. Provide Clean Water

Birds should have access to clean drinking water. Check drinkers regularly and keep them clean to reduce contamination.

3. Store Feed Properly

Store feed in a cool, dry, and clean location. Protect bags from moisture, pests, and direct contact with the floor.

4. Reduce Feed Waste

Avoid filling feeders excessively. Monitor spilled feed and adjust feeder height and quantity when necessary.

5. Monitor Bird Growth

Regularly observe bird weight, activity, appetite, and general condition. Changes can help farmers identify feeding or management problems early.

6. Make Changes Gradually

When changing feed, avoid sudden changes where possible. Gradual changes can make it easier for birds to adjust to a new feeding program.

Managing Feed Costs

Feed is one of the major expenses in poultry production. Farmers can improve cost management by reducing unnecessary waste, comparing ingredient prices, buying quality ingredients, and keeping accurate records.

The cheapest ingredient is not always the best choice. A good feeding program should consider nutritional value, ingredient quality, bird performance, and the overall cost of producing healthy birds.

Keeping simple records of feed purchased, feed consumed, bird numbers, growth, and production can help a farmer identify changes in performance and make better decisions.
